Whats is Semantic-tags

A semantic element clearly describes its meaning to both the browser and the developer. Examples of non-semantic elements: <div> and <span> - Tells nothing about its content. Examples of semantic elements: <img>, <table>, and <article> - Clearly defines its content.</p>
